version: 0.1.20-preview.24
date: 2026-11-22
title: Smoother recordings and review playback
---

- Smoother captured video: after dropping a torn frame, Greyscreen now publishes the next frame right away instead of holding the previous one, and restricts the per-tile tear check to the HUD so combat flashes are kept.
- Cursor-only and unchanged frames from Windows capture now merge into the current frame instead of taking up extra 60 fps slots, removing hidden frame holds.
- Smoother playback on high-refresh displays: the Windows review player now waits on a high-resolution timer and snaps video presents to your display's refresh grid.
- Quitting Greyscreen from the system tray now finishes and saves any in-progress recording cleanly before exiting.
- Delayed NVIDIA encoder teardown and moved recording-start disk work off the capture control thread to prevent driver crashes on quick restarts and hitches on slow drives.
- Late-starting game audio now stays in sync across finished recordings, trims, and exported clips.
