date: 2026-11-21
title: Recording health and cleaner captures
---

- Each recording now saves a health summary: frame rate, dropped frames, sound gaps and sync drift. Open it from the review toolbar.
- Turn on Settings → Recording → Detailed recording diagnostics to log every second of the next game for troubleshooting.
- New `greyscreen-cli analyze <folder>` checks a recording and gives a verdict per area.
- Greyscreen now drops the half-drawn frames Windows sometimes captures, which showed as brief flashes without the HUD.
- The Settings window grows on larger screens.
- The Check for updates button no longer changes size while checking.
- The search clear button now clears the search.
