---
version: 0.1.20-preview.23
date: 2026-11-22
title: More reliable recording and clearer errors
---

- Recordings now stop normally and keep the video if audio recording fails during a game, instead of hanging forever.
- Recording errors such as a full disk now show up right away during a game instead of waiting until the match ends, with a "disk full" message when storage runs out. Clips keep working.
- Windows no longer puts your PC to sleep or turns off the display while a recording is running.
- Greyscreen no longer freezes while waiting for a slow game capture to start. If starting times out, nothing is left running in the background.
- A faulty graphics driver can no longer freeze or crash Greyscreen during the hardware encoder check, which now runs in isolation with a time limit. Greyscreen also tells you when another app is already using your graphics card's encoder, and Home shows the first line of the encoder error.
- Clips exported for Discord now keep the same colour information as recordings, so their colours show correctly in players.
