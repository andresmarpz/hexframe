title: Steadier recordings when the game is busy
---

- Fixed recordings that could show a frame out of order while the game kept the graphics card busy.
- Detailed recording diagnostics now note each second how busy the graphics card and its video encoder were, and `greyscreen-cli analyze` reports how many dropped frames happened while the card was full.
