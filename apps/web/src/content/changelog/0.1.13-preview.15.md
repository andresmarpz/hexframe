title: Clip export, first run and a sturdier recorder
---

- Export a clip or any moment of a game: Original quality, or a version under 10 MB for Discord. Copy file pastes it straight into Discord.
- If your GPU's encoder fails, Greyscreen falls back to Windows' software encoder instead of not recording. Settings shows which encoder is in use.
- Recording problems now say what happened and what to do, with a link to the FAQ: Fullscreen instead of Borderless, folder not writable, low disk and more.
- A short first-run card helps you pick a folder and quality and run a 10-second test recording.
- Recordings cut off by a crash or power loss are repaired the next time Greyscreen starts.
- Greyscreen won't start recording when the disk is nearly full, and stops cleanly before it fills up.
- The tray menu can open Greyscreen, pause recording, save a clip and quit; its icon shows when it's recording.
