title: Fast, smooth scrubbing
---

- Dragging the seek bar no longer freezes the video: it shows quick previews while you drag and plays from the new spot as soon as you let go.
- Long jumps, like ten minutes forward or back, land much faster.
- If playback ever stalls, Greyscreen writes a short report to its log so the cause can be found.
