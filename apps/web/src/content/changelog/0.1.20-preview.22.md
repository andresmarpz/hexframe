version: 0.1.20-preview.22
date: 2026-11-22
title: Collapsible Review panels and cleaner Settings
---

- Review panels can now collapse out of the way: toggle the Games list with `G`, the Events/Clips panel with `I`, windowed Theater mode with `T`, or fullscreen with `F`. The app also opens in a larger 1280x760 window by default.
- Cleaned up the Review toolbar: Export now lives in the player bar, while Recording health, Show in folder, and Delete sit in the more menu with an alert dot if a recording has an issue. The Theater drawer now has Events and Clips tabs, and keys `1` through `7` toggle Death Review tags.
- Reorganized Settings into five tabs (General, Recording, Clips & Review, Storage, Keybinds) with search in the left sidebar and click-to-record shortcut pills in Keybinds.
- Clips now appear in a responsive 3-column grid with search. You can also click the Last game card or View all clips on Home, click Storage in the sidebar to jump to Storage settings, and open Games with your newest match already selected.
- Steadier layout across the app: the auto-hiding Dock no longer bounces the Review video, Export and Feedback dialogs keep steady sizes and button widths while working, and the update banner sits in the bottom-right corner without covering Home.
