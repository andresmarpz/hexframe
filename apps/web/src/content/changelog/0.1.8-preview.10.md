title: New design with Home, Games and Clips
---

- New design: Home, Games and Clips screens, with your games listed beside the review in one window.
- Pick a sidebar or a floating dock under Settings → Recording → Navigation style.
- The sidebar shows the recorder status, your last 5 games and storage, and folds to a rail with Ctrl+\.
- The dock widens while recording to show the timer and a Clip button, and hides while a video plays.
- Review deaths walks through each death with auto pause and slow motion, and saves tags and notes for each death.
- Game rows show how many deaths you have reviewed.
- Thumbnails for every game and clip, and a Clips screen with all your clips.
- Right-click a game for Open, Review deaths, Show in folder and Delete.
- Settings opens as a window with five sections and a search box.
- A banner tells you when an update is ready, with a Restart button.
- Shortcuts: Ctrl+1, 2 and 3 open Home, Games and Clips; Up and Down step through games; Ctrl+F searches.
