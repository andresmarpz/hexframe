- Reviews on Windows play sound again; before, sound stopped after a split second.
- Pausing near the end of a game no longer leaves the video stuck.
- Event markers on the seek bar sit at the right place instead of piling up at the start.
- Start Greyscreen with GREYSCREEN_VIDEO_LOG=1 to write a detailed playback log for bug reports.
