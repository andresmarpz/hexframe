title: Smooth playback, event filters and sounds
---

- Pausing holds the picture where it is, sound stays in sync with the video, and seeking to the very end no longer gets stuck.
- Click the video to play or pause; double-click for theater mode.
- Filter which events show in a review and on the seek bar, with a default in Settings → Review and a per-game override.
- KDA, deaths and Review deaths now work in modes where League names players by champion.
- Updating no longer crashes the installer, so Greyscreen reopens after an update and the setup window finishes.
- Deleting a game that is open in the review now works, and shows a message if Windows still refuses.
- Short, quiet sounds for recording started, recording saved, clip saved and errors, with a switch and volume in Settings.
- Games over an hour show their full length, like 1:15:30.
